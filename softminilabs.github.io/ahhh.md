## 气到不行的时候，打开它，对着手机喊一嗓子。

满屏的字——加班、考试、前任的名字——像纸贴纸一样砸下来，把小人埋住。你喊得越大声，炸得越猛：字被炸飞、纸屑四溅、手机跟着震。喊完那一刻，整个人都松了。

### 怎么玩

- 点「开始发泄」，想炸的字从天而降
- 对着手机大喊，音量越大，炸掉的字越多
- 拍个手、打个响指、吹声口哨，也能引爆
- 不方便出声？点「手动爽一下」，按住蓄力松手开炸
- 炸光一波，再来一波，直到爽够为止

### 想炸啥写啥

内置通用、上班族、学生党、情感四组词库；也可以自己输入——老板的名字、那句咽不下去的话，写上去，亲手炸掉。摇一摇手机，随机换一个。

### 五只纸艺搭子

土拨鼠、鳄鱼、打工人、小火山、纸箱人，双击小人就能换。换到纸箱人时，试试长按它——有惊喜。

### 喊完，好好收个尾

没有分数、没有排名、不算成绩。发泄完只有一句话陪你收尾——深呼吸，碎的是它，不是你。想分享的话，一张纸艺卡片把「今天炸掉的坏心情」发给朋友。

### 安心喊

- 麦克风只用来检测音量和识别拍手口哨，不录音、不上传
- 完全离线，不联网、不注册

适合开完会想骂人的你、赶完论文的你、被已读不回气到的你。关起车门、蒙上被子，喊就完了。

## When you are about to explode, open this app and yell.

The screen fills with paper-sticker words — overtime, exams, your ex — piling up and burying a little paper buddy. Yell at your phone: the louder you are, the bigger the blast. Words pop, confetti flies, your phone rumbles. When the last word is gone, you feel lighter.

### How it works

- Tap Start Venting and watch the words rain down
- Yell at your phone. Louder voice, bigger blast
- Clap, snap your fingers or whistle — that blasts too
- Can't make noise right now? Hold the manual button to charge, release to blast
- Clear a wave, call the next one, repeat until you feel good

### Blast any word

Four built-in word packs: Everyday, Work Life, Student Life, Love & Hate. Or type your own — your boss's name, that one sentence you can't let go — and blow it up yourself. Shake your phone for a random word.

### Five paper buddies

A marmot, a crocodile, an office worker, a little volcano and a box-head man. Double-tap to switch. When you get the box-head man, try holding him down — there is a surprise.

### A calm ending

No scores, no rankings, no grades. When you are done, one quiet line helps you land: deep breath — it shattered, you didn't. Want to share? Send friends a paper-craft card of the bad mood you just destroyed.

### Yell without worry

- The microphone only measures volume and recognizes claps or whistles. Nothing is recorded or uploaded
- Works fully offline, no sign-up

For anyone who just left a terrible meeting, submitted a thesis at 3 AM, or got left on read. Roll up the car windows, grab a pillow, and let it out.
