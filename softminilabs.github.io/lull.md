## 专为睡眠、专注和放松打造的环境音 App。

界面简洁，没有多余功能。打开就能用，点击播放，放下手机。

### 62 种精调环境音

自然、城市、工业噪音、Lo-Fi 音乐、双耳节拍——涵盖各类需求。每一段音频都经过精心设计，循环无缝衔接，加入自然变奏，听不出重复感。

### 混音编辑器，随意叠加

屋檐雨声 + 咖啡馆环境音 + Lo-Fi 钢琴，组成你专属的声景。保存混音，下次一键加载。

### 场景世界，走进声音里

五个完全动态的沉浸场景，随你激活的声音而变化。不是静态壁纸，是一片真正活着的环境。

- 篝火之夜：松林深处的篝火，蟋蟀低鸣，溪水潺潺（免费）
- 极光之夜：北极地平线上流淌的绿色极光，搭配海浪声或 Delta 双耳节拍
- 雨夜城市：紫色调的城市雨夜，毛毛雨、小雨、大雨、雷雨四种强度
- 雪夜寂静：深冬的静谧，雪花飘落，木屋窗里透出暖光
- 萤火虫夜：盛夏草地上的夜晚，荷叶上的青蛙，蝉鸣与溪水声

### 定时渐出，安静停止

设置 5 分钟到数小时的倒计时。开启渐出模式，音量在结束前缓缓降低，不会突然中断惊醒你。

### 睡眠计算器

告诉眠静你需要几点起床，它按 90 分钟睡眠周期倒推最佳入睡时间，起床不再迷糊。

### 专为夜晚设计

全深色界面，没有刺眼的白色。锁屏与主屏小组件，不用解锁手机就能播放和暂停。

免费版包含全部基础声音、每个混音最多 2 条音轨和篝火场景。眠静 Premium 一次买断，永久享用全部 62 种声音、无限混音与全部场景，未来新增声音永久免费获得。无广告、无账号、不收集任何数据。

## An ambient sound app built for one thing: sleep, focus, unwind.

No clutter. Tap play and put the phone down.

### 62 hand-crafted ambient sounds

Nature, city, industrial, Lo-Fi music and binaural beats. Every sound loops seamlessly with natural variation — it never sounds like a loop.

### Layer sounds in the mixer

Combine rain on a roof with a coffee-shop hum and a Lo-Fi piano. Save your mix and load it with one tap tomorrow night.

### Scene World — step inside the sound

Five fully animated worlds that respond to the sounds you play. Each scene is a living environment, not a static wallpaper.

- Campfire: crackling fire under a pine-forest sky, crickets and a cool stream (free)
- Aurora: shimmering northern lights over an arctic sea, perfect with ocean waves
- Rainy Night: a purple city in the rain; choose drizzle, rain, downpour or thunderstorm
- Snowy Night: falling snow, chimney smoke and warm cabin windows
- Firefly Night: a summer meadow, frogs on lily pads and drifting fireflies

### Sleep timer with gentle fade-out

Set 5 minutes to several hours. With Fade Out on, the volume lowers slowly before stopping — no jarring silence to wake you.

### Sleep cycle calculator

Tell Lull when you need to wake up and it suggests the ideal bedtime, working backwards through 90-minute sleep cycles.

### Made for the dark

A true night-first interface with no blinding whites. Start and stop playback from the Lock Screen or Home Screen widgets without unlocking your phone.

Free includes all basic sounds, up to 2 tracks per mix and the Campfire scene. Lull Premium is a one-time purchase: all 62 sounds, unlimited mixes, all five scenes and every future sound. No ads, no account, no data collection.
