## 把一串截图变成一张看不出接缝的长图。

选好顺序，重叠部分自动去除，几秒钟拼好，高清导出。聊天记录留存、网课笔记整理、商品详情长图、社交平台发布——需要把截图连成一张的时刻，长图拼接都能又快又稳地搞定。

### 无缝拼接

- 自动识别相邻截图的重叠区域，去重并对齐
- 每处接缝可拖动微调，未识别时也能手动调整
- 单图独立裁剪顶部 / 底部，去掉重复的导航栏、输入框，还能一键应用到中间所有图
- 检测到状态栏自动提示，一键清理
- 支持竖向 / 横向拼接，长按拖动排序

### 智能检测

- 打开 App 自动发现刚截的连续截图，一键拼接
- 只读取元数据判断，不扫描图片内容
- 未授权相册也不影响：系统选择器零权限直达

### 网页截长图

输入或粘贴链接，内置浏览器分段截取整页，直接生成一张完整的网页长图。

### 创意拼图

18 款模板：九宫格、四宫格、六宫格、瀑布流、宝丽来、胶片风、同心圆、花瓣、四叶草、爱心、拼图块、胶囊、镜面反射、时间轴、蜂窝、楼梯、扇形放射、照片夹。点选即可更换、删除，或交换两张照片的位置。

### 高清导出与清理

- JPG、PNG 无损、PDF 三种格式，默认原始分辨率，绝不静默压缩
- 超长图片可智能分段保存，避免发出去变糊
- 保存成功后一键删除原始截图，经系统确认后执行，可在「最近删除」中找回

### 简单放心

全程在本机完成，不上传任何图片、不消耗流量。无广告、无水印。

## A pile of screenshots, one long image with no visible seams.

Pick your shots, set the order, and the overlapping parts are removed automatically. Chat histories, lecture notes, product pages, receipts — whenever screenshots need to become one image, Long Stitch does it fast and does it cleanly.

### Seamless stitching

- Detects the overlap between neighboring screenshots and aligns them automatically
- Fine-tune every seam with a slider whenever you want more control
- Trim the top and bottom of each image to remove repeated nav bars, and apply the same crop to all middle images at once
- Status bar detected? Clean it up with one tap
- Vertical or horizontal stitching, drag to reorder

### Smart detection

- Open the app and your latest burst of consecutive screenshots is already waiting
- Detection reads only metadata, never the content of your photos
- No photo permission? The system picker still gets you there

### Web capture

Paste a link and the built-in browser captures the whole page in segments. Get a complete web page as a single long image.

### Collage

18 templates: 3×3 grid, 2×2 grid, hero grid, waterfall, polaroid, film strip, concentric, petals, clover, heart, jigsaw, capsules, reflection, timeline, honeycomb, stairs, radial and clothesline. Tap to replace, remove, or swap any two photos.

### Export and clean up

- JPG, lossless PNG or PDF, at original resolution by default
- Very long images can be saved in segments so they stay sharp everywhere
- After saving, delete the original screenshots in one step — confirmed by the system and recoverable from Recently Deleted

### Private by design

Everything happens on your device — nothing is ever uploaded. No ads, no watermark.
