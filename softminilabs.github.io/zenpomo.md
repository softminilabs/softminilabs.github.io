## 一款为学习、写作、阅读与深度工作设计的专注计时器。

它不只是一个番茄钟，更帮助你减少分心、进入节奏，并用清晰的专注统计、趋势图和热力图，把专注慢慢变成稳定的日常习惯。

### 开始专注，更少阻力

- 一键开始专注或休息
- 自定义专注与休息时长
- 支持自动开始下一个计时阶段
- 用标签记录不同类型的专注内容
- 通过声音、界面与节奏，建立更适合自己的专注流程

### 减少分心，保护你的时间

- 屏蔽容易分心的应用
- 开启严格模式，在专注期间减少中断
- 保持屏幕常亮，让计时过程更稳定
- 支持通知提醒与锁屏实时状态展示

### 让努力真正看得见

- 查看今日、每周、每月、每年的专注统计数据
- 用 24 小时分布找到更适合你的高效时段
- 用热力图回顾专注节奏与连续性
- 用分类占比了解时间花在了哪里

### 把专注带到系统界面

桌面小组件、锁屏小组件与实时活动，倒计时、今日数据与趋势统计一目了然。

### Zen Premium 可解锁更多能力

高级主题与更完整的个性化外观、更多环境音与沉浸式体验、iCloud 同步，以及后续更新内容。如果你正在寻找一款界面安静、功能清晰、又真正能帮助你减少分心并长期坚持的专注应用，番茄钟会是一个更稳定的选择。

## A focus timer built for study, reading, writing, and deep work.

It does more than count down time. It helps you block distractions, settle into a better rhythm, and build a steadier focus habit with clear stats, trends, and heatmaps.

### Start focusing with less friction

- Start focus and break sessions in one tap
- Customize focus and break durations
- Automatically move to the next timer stage
- Use tags to organize different kinds of focus sessions
- Build a better rhythm with sound, structure, and pacing

### Reduce distractions and protect your time

- Block distracting apps during focus sessions
- Use Strict Mode to reduce interruptions
- Keep the screen awake while you focus
- Use reminders and Live Activities to stay on track

### See your progress clearly

- Review your focus data by day, week, month, and year
- Find your most productive hours with the 24-hour distribution
- Use heatmaps to review your focus rhythm and consistency
- Use category breakdowns to understand where your time goes

### Bring focus to your system surfaces

Home Screen widgets, Lock Screen widgets and Live Activities — countdowns, today's stats and trends at a glance.

### Zen Premium unlocks more

Premium themes and deeper personalization, more ambient sounds, iCloud sync, and future updates. If you want a focus app that feels calm, clear, and genuinely helps you stay consistent, this one is built for you.
