## 今年你订阅了几个 App？每月被扣多少钱？

订况帮你看清每一笔订阅。不绑银行卡、不碰你的隐私，数据只存在你的 iPhone 和 iCloud 里。支持一次买断，从此告别「管理订阅的 App 自己也要订阅」的怪圈。

### 为什么选订况

- 隐私第一：不连银行、零分析埋点、零第三方 SDK，App Store 隐私标签为「不收集数据」
- 一次买断：一次解锁，终身使用全部功能
- 美如小票：灵感来自 POS 热敏打印机，一键把订阅账单「打印」成可撕下分享的小票
- Apple 原生：SwiftUI 打造，iCloud 同步、桌面小组件、Apple Watch 复杂功能一应俱全

### 主要功能

- 订阅记录不限量（免费版 3 条）
- 试用期倒计时，到期前自动提醒，再也不忘取消
- 周 / 月 / 季 / 年 / 自定义天数周期
- 30+ 币种自动汇率换算
- iCloud 多设备同步（iPhone / iPad）
- 桌面小组件，小 / 中 / 大三种尺寸
- 到期提醒提前 1 / 3 / 7 天，支持自定义
- 月历视图 + 支出统计，哪天扣款一目了然
- CSV 导入导出，数据随时带走
- 深色模式，9 种语言

### 我们不做什么

不连银行账户、不卖数据、没有广告、不强迫订阅。

## How many apps did you subscribe to this year?

See every dollar in one place — without giving any third-party tool your bank credentials. All your data lives only on your iPhone and iCloud. Pay once, and stop paying a monthly fee for the app that tracks your monthly fees.

### Why Subli

- Privacy-first: no bank link, zero analytics, zero third-party SDKs
- Pay once: unlock every feature for life with a single purchase
- Beautiful by design: inspired by POS thermal printers — "print" your subscription bill into a torn-paper receipt and share it
- Native Apple: SwiftUI, iCloud sync, Home Screen widgets, Apple Watch complications

### Features

- Unlimited subscriptions (free up to 3)
- Trial countdown and reminders — never forget to cancel
- Weekly, monthly, quarterly, yearly, or custom-day cycles
- 30+ currencies with automatic conversion
- iCloud sync across iPhone and iPad
- Home Screen widgets in three sizes
- Renewal alerts at 1, 3, 7 days, or any custom day
- Calendar view and spending stats — see exactly when money leaves
- CSV import and export — your data goes wherever you go
- Dark mode, 9 languages

### What we don't do

No bank linking. No data selling. No ads. No subscription pressure.
