## 别忘了在乎的人。

念念是一个温柔的记事 App，专门帮你记住对你重要的人，和他们值得被记住的日子。不是 Todo，不是日历——是一张张手写的小卡片，到点亮起来。

### 把生日、纪念日、节气写下来

支持公历和农历。妈妈的农历生日、外婆的祭日、和恋人在一起的第几天——一次设好，每年自动提醒。

### 手绘风格的卡片，每个人一张

15 种水墨手绘插画，按关系或心情选一张。家人、朋友、爱人、宠物，每个人在念念里都有自己的样子。不是冷冰冰的列表。

### 桌面小组件，一眼就看到

锁屏和桌面小组件，把最近要到的日子直接显示出来。不用打开 App 也能记起。

### 联系记录，关系不会褪色

记录每一次和某个人的见面、通话、信息。哪些朋友很久没联系了，念念默默替你看着。

### 情感账单，每月一张

月底自动生成一张「情感账单」，告诉你这个月谁陪你最多，你欠谁一通电话。可以保存为图片分享。

### 专为温柔设计

纸质感的米色界面，手写的字体，没有刺眼的红点徽章，没有让人焦虑的待办未读数。打开就是平静。

免费版包含最多 3 个提醒、10 条联系记录、公历与农历提醒和当月情感账单。念念 Pro 解锁无限提醒与记录、历史账单回看与导出、桌面小组件和 iCloud 同步，月度、年度、永久买断三档可选。

## A quiet, handwritten reminder app for the people who matter.

Not a to-do list. Not a calendar. Just a small ink-drawn card for each person, lighting up the day before something important.

### Birthdays, anniversaries, the day you first met

Add anyone. Pick a date. Pick solar or lunar — the lunar calendar is built in, for grandmothers, for Lunar New Year babies, for anyone whose birthday is the 15th of the 8th moon.

### Hand-drawn cards, one per person

15 ink illustrations — choose one that fits the relationship. Mom, partner, best friend, the dog. Everyone gets their own little portrait. No cold list rows.

### Home Screen and Lock Screen widgets

Surface what's coming this week without unlocking. A small widget for the one person you care about most; a medium widget for the next four upcoming days.

### Contact log — relationships don't fade silently

Tap a person, log a call, a coffee, a visit. Days Since keeps the timeline quietly in the background, so you can see who you haven't talked to in a while.

### Monthly Relationship Receipt

At the end of every month, the app prints you a paper receipt: who you spent time with, who you owe a call. Saveable as an image, shareable if you want.

### Designed to be gentle

Paper-cream interface, hand-drawn typeface, no red badges, no streak shame. Just open it, see what's coming, close it.

Free includes up to 3 reminders, 10 contact log entries, solar and lunar reminders and this month's receipt. Pro unlocks unlimited reminders and entries, historical receipts, sharing, widgets and iCloud sync — monthly, yearly, or lifetime.
